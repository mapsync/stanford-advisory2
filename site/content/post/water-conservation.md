🚨💧 URGENT WATER CONSERVATION NOTICE 💧🚨


Due to the continued lack of adequate rainfall and the current dry conditions, our lake levels are currently low and we are asking all of our customers to take immediate steps to help us conserve water in order to extend our current lake supply until rainfall picks up.
Our water supply is a critical resource, and we need your help NOW to reduce unnecessary water usage while these dry conditions continue. Every drop counts!


⚠️ PLEASE TAKE IMMEDIATE ACTION NOW. 



Some easy steps that you can take to help conserve our water supply:
💧 Stop all unnecessary outdoor watering, including lawns and landscaping.
🚿 Take shorter showers and limit water usage whenever possible.
🚰 Turn off faucets when not in use.
🧺 Only run washing machines and dishwashers with full loads.
🚗 Avoid washing vehicles, pressure washing, and other nonessential water use.



PLEASE DO NOT TAKE OUR WATER SUPPLY FOR GRANTED. Without adequate rainfall, continued high water usage can put additional strain on our water system and possibly cause us to enter into an emergency situation.



We are urging every household and business to make water conservation a priority immediately. Even small changes can make a significant difference when our entire community works together.


Thank you for your immediate attention and cooperation..
